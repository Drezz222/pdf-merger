PDF merger FAQ

Can I reorder individual PDF pages?

Yes. After loading your files, the visual workspace lets you drag individual page thumbnails into a new order before exporting.

Can I combine images with PDFs?

Yes. The tool supports common image formats including JPG, PNG, WEBP, TIFF and BMP alongside PDF files.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to use the merger.

Can I remove pages before merging?

Yes. Remove unwanted pages from the preview, then merge the remaining pages into your finished PDF.
