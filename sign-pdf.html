Sign PDF FAQ

Does my local PDF need to be uploaded?

Files selected directly from your device are processed in the browser. Remote URL or Drive imports must first be fetched into the workspace.

Can I sign more than one page?

Yes. Navigate between pages and place a signature on each page that needs one.

Is this a certificate-based digital signature?

No. This feature adds a visual electronic signature to the PDF. It does not create a cryptographic certificate signature.
